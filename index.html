About the Developer & Why KeyType Pro Exists

Crafted for Students, Stenographers & Future Civil Servants

Every year, thousands of bright and dedicated candidates clear tough written prelims and mains examinations, only to face anxiety during the final qualifying Typing Skill Test (DEST / CPT) due to lack of realistic practice software.

I developed KeyType Pro (Typing Master Ultra) to bridge this gap completely. Unlike basic typing websites that only show raw WPM, this platform combines Strict Exam Hall Simulation, Bilingual Hindi (Unicode/Kruti Dev) & English Support, AI Weak-Key Detection, Finger-Guided Virtual Keyboards, and Verifiable Photo Certificates in one seamless, inspiring workspace.
